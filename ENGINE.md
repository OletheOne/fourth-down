# Fourth Down decision engine v3

This is a bounded, deterministic-seed decision model, not a guarantee or a trained win-probability model.

Analysis ships as an inline worker so startup does not depend on another authenticated asset request. If workers are blocked, fail to load, or fail to reply within 30 seconds, the same bundled engine runs locally as a fallback. Cancelled/profile-switched jobs cannot publish results. Refresh requests include credentials and check status/content type before parsing JSON. Expired private-site sessions offer a top-level Sites sign-in link; failed refreshes do not replace players, keepers or picks. No API key or authentication bypass token is bundled in the client.

## Objective and rollout

Score completed rosters by optimized starter points, bye-week replacement coverage, diminishing bench insurance above waiver replacement, and (keeper mode only) discounted keeper portfolio value. Subtract a configurable downside preference times scenario dispersion. Shared random scenarios compare a shortlist built from immediate roster gains, market leaders, each position's leader and keeper candidates. Opponents use their own roster needs, limits, ADP dispersion and recent position runs. Later user picks use greedy legal roster improvement. This is full-draft rollout with a bounded shortlist, not exhaustive search over every possible draft sequence.

The default ordering is the immediate roster-value policy. Historical evaluation did not establish an advantage from rollouts, so an evidence gate prevents them overriding the default. Experimental lookahead is an explicit Setup choice, using a paired conservative comparison to the baseline. Future keeper option values are displayed but do not change the default recommendation; select the experimental mode to optimize that speculative objective. Raw model values are not percentages and need not follow the selected ordering. Availability estimates are conditional on the supplied board, not calibrated probabilities. Recommendation confidence remains low because real-world superiority is unvalidated.

Both simulated drafting choices AND simulated lineup selection use observable projections only. Latent performance perturbations grade an already-selected lineup; they cannot influence future choices or produce hindsight-optimal starters. Paired outcome scenarios are a sensitivity assumption, not calibrated NFL distributions.

## Lineups, data and league rules

Dedicated slots are assigned first, then RB/WR/TE FLEX, then QB/RB/WR/TE superflex. Every player occupies at most one slot. Roster capacity and position limits reserve room for mandatory unfilled starters. Waiver replacement is derived from league-wide positional demand including benches. Bye overlap loses projected weekly coverage; usable reserves provide cover. Bench insurance uses a disclosed 12% option-value assumption with diminishing returns.

Provider scoring totals are applied exactly once. Stat-coefficient overrides adjust their known baseline, including per-position premiums and K/DST coefficients. Missing statistics, unverified zero milestone projections and scoring mismatches are explicit. FG-distance and points-allowed counts now have a separately trained historical model (see below). Estimated contributions are separated from provider totals. These customized projections are not exact event forecasts.

The dedicated 2026 Discord keeper profile uses the user's ESPN screenshots: 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 2 FLEX, 1 DST, no kicker, 6 bench and 1 IR. IR does not add a draft slot: 15 rounds total. Position maximums are QB 4, RB 8, WR 8, TE 3, DST 3, K 0. These supersede the earlier FantasyPros roster import. Existing scoring remains standard, -2 interceptions, 2 points per defensive sack and 10/7/4/1/0/-1/-4 points-allowed bands; no new scoring screenshot was supplied.

All 32 selected keepers and exact current-year costs are embedded in `lib/keeper-preset.ts`; four teams have an unused third keeper slot. All 32 assignments independently agree with the inferred snake order, with Super Smash Burrows in slot 8. Nico Collins reserves R6/P5 (#65), Rashee Rice R12/P5 (#137). Finalized costs are not escalated again. Missing feed identities get explicitly unprojected keeper-only placeholders until matched; Jr/Sr/III aliases are reconciled without fabricated projections. Live sync can merge valid nonkeeper picks but cannot replace screenshot settings, order or keeper contracts. Traditional drafts use separate saved profiles and retain editable settings. The original local Discord state is backed up before migration, and any traditional draft formerly stored there is retained as a separate profile.

Earlier benchmark reports retain their original as-tested configurations; they do not establish an advantage for this corrected roster or preset. Future keeper-value edge cases remain assumptions, not commissioner-confirmed facts.

The player catalog remains available for identity reconciliation, but inactive catalog-only players and missing/zero current projections are not recommended. Newest news wins; injuries use the actual injuries response array. ADP average and dispersion, expert spread and ranking range, season, fetch time, ranking time and projection time are retained when provided. Unknown source timestamps remain unknown. Designations never invent missed games. Recent availability/role news triggers review and uncertainty; a sourced user override can model absence without double-counting it in projections. News is a limited recent feed, not a complete dossier.

Confirmed current-draft roster snapshots contribute roster context and remove players from availability. Unconfirmed/prior-season snapshots do not. Roster-only players without pick numbers cause a timing warning. Name normalization reconciles punctuation/case, and duplicate/unmatched identities remain visible. User-entered picks and keepers are retained during feed refresh and profile changes invalidate in-flight refreshes.

## Keeper economics

Defaults: three keepers, one round earlier per year, three future years, 0.65 annual discount. Contend/balanced/rebuild discounts future option value by an additional 0.15/0.30/0.50 objective weight. These are disclosed modeling preferences, not fitted coefficients. The round-one boundary and collision rule must be confirmed with the commissioner.

Future value is inferred from dynasty positional consensus and current positional projection curves, not a claimed future ADP forecast. No separate age penalty duplicates dynasty consensus. Enumerate initial keeper portfolios and, for up to three contracts, all cost-assignment orders. Future years can only retain/drop those players; actual charged collision costs carry forward. Larger keeper limits use deterministic future-value cost ordering. The model does not assume a player who was dropped can reappear as a keeper. Traditional mode has no dynasty/keeper component regardless of team direction.

## Historical bonus model

`research/acquire.py` retrieves nflverse weekly player/team data (2018–2025) and game scores, recording hashes and source URLs. `research/bonus_model.py` creates `lib/bonus-model.json` and chronological validation reports. Data attribution: [nflverse](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Transformations: distance-bin frequencies and defensive points-allowed distributions. The runtime artifact is fitted through 2025 and explicitly refuses other projection seasons than 2026.

Development uses 2020–2022 rolling forecasts, always trained on earlier seasons. Shrinkage is selected by categorical Brier score. Kicker-specific histories lost to the recent league prior: the deployed kicker model therefore uses a league distribution (approximately 54/26/20% for 0–39/40–49/50+). Multiply by the provider's made-FG projection and replace its flat three-point FG contribution with configured total band points. DST uses heavily shrunk team frequencies (128 prior games), multiplied by 17 games or a supplied expected-games projection.

Before adding either estimate, reconstruct the provider's standard K/DST baseline from projected components and require agreement within 0.15 points. If definitions change or the provider starts including these bands, fail closed with a warning instead of double counting. Custom flat-FG coefficients are replaced by the total band weights, not added twice. Generic milestone fields remain unsupported where no validated model exists.

2023–2025 bonus evaluation: kicker season MAE 3.02/3.79/3.80 points versus omitted-bonus errors 13.38/14.11/15.00; this is **conditional on actual made-FG volume**, not a validation of FantasyPros FG-volume projections. DST season MAE 11.29/12.03/11.84 versus omitted-band errors 17.44/12.63/15.03. DST advantage over a league prior is small/mixed, not proof of strong team-specific predictive skill. Game scores exclude opponent defensive TDs, safeties and defensive conversion returns; this reconstruction has not been independently reconciled with ESPN game logs. Schedule strength and personnel changes are not modeled. Custom weights can change the error substantially. Historical MAE is not a per-player confidence interval.

## Verification and limits

Run optimizer scenario tests, the feed adapter tests, the first-pick synthetic benchmark, TypeScript checking, production build, and worker bundle test. The optional live-optimizer test uses a runtime-only authorized key and prints no credentials.

The old 96-case first-pick synthetic exercise is retired as evidence: it shared follow-up policies, treated clustered fixtures as independent, and was revisited during development. It remains only a regression test. Read `research/PROTOCOL.md` for the pre-result protocol and its explicit audit amendment.

The replacement historical benchmark uses 12 teams × 15 rounds and makes every user pick under ECR, roster value or experimental lookahead. Three slots and two opponent styles across 2024/2025 and PPR/standard yield 24 contexts and 72 complete policy runs with legal rosters. Actual weekly points are hidden from draft policies and weekly lineup selection; known byes are permitted. Preseason rank-to-points models train on earlier years only. Ranked busts are retained, and all top-180 identities matched.

In 2025 lookahead trailed roster value by 102.60 PPR points and 143.68 standard points on average. 2024 differences were -9.30 and +30.72. The 2024 run was partially observed before a hindsight-grading bug fix, so it is an audited replay, NOT an untouched holdout. Only 2025 remained untouched for the final policy. One season cannot establish general superiority, regardless of how many draft slots are replayed. The default gate follows the predeclared preference for the simpler policy when superiority is not established; the failed experimental strategy remains accessible with disclosure.

Archived rankings: [DynastyProcess](https://github.com/dynastyprocess/data), documented by [nflreadr](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/reference/load_ff_rankings.html). Raw archived rankings/IDs and their derived forecast fixtures are used only in ignored local research files, not distributed in the runtime model. This archive contains PPR ECR, NOT ADP; its latest 2025 preseason snapshot is August 8. The standard-scoring run is a stress test against a PPR ranking market, not proof of beating correctly configured standard ECR. Historical forecasts are estimated from earlier rank/outcome relationships, not archived FantasyPros projections. Simulated opponents, frozen preseason lineups, no waivers/injury updates and no historical keeper evaluation limit applicability. See aggregate research reports for all results, including failures.

League confirmation, optional source absences, stale data, assumed uncertainty, missing exact scoring inputs and unsupported draft types (auction, traded-pick schedules, IDP, non-nested flex eligibility) require human review. This release supports the requested snake traditional/three-keeper draft; it must not silently claim support for other formats.
